This folder contains the escape profiles for each of the 82 antibodies we studied.

Results are summarized in Supplementary_Data_epitopes.pptx, where a heatmap is provided for every antibody. Barcharts above the heatmaps give the average escape score across a site (irrespective of the amino acid mutated to). If the site has a score at least one standard deviation above the mean, and at least 8 of the per-variant scores at that site are one standard deviation above the mean, then those sites are highlightd with a red star. These sites are used to infer the epitope and important secondary features.

Raw results are provided for each antibody in .csv format.
