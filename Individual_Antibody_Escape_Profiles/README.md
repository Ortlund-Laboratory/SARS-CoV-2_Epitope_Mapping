This folder contains the escape profiles for each of the antibodies we studied.

Raw results are provided for each antibody in .csv format.

We used a custom script, all_heatmaps_generation.R, to generate heatmaps and barcharts for each of these antibodies. This script requires a reference.csv file, which is also provided.

Results are summarized in Supplementary_Data_epitopes.pptx (available from the supplemental materials published alongside our manuscript), where a heatmap is provided for every antibody. Barcharts above the heatmaps give the average escape score across a site (irrespective of the amino acid mutated to). If the site has a score at least one standard deviation above the mean, and at least 8 of the per-variant scores at that site are one standard deviation above the mean, then those sites are highlightd with a red star. These sites are used to infer the epitope and important secondary features.
